The challenge

Building the SEO foundation, not just publishing more content.

TestDino needed a structured organic search operation for a developer-focused SaaS product. The website had limited established content and lacked a mature site structure.

The work extended beyond keyword targeting: establish site structure, identify relevant search demand, coordinate content production, maintain technical SEO, and make sure published content accurately reflected the product.

The priorities

  • check_circleEstablish a usable site structure and content foundation.
  • check_circleFind relevant search topics with demonstrable demand.
  • check_circleCoordinate five content writers through a consistent editorial process.
  • check_circleMaintain technical SEO with the web developer.
  • check_circleImprove production efficiency without leaving factual accuracy behind.
Strategy

Four connected parts of the work.

A demand-led plan, a workable site, a coordinated editorial operation, and continuous improvement.

01 / Demand

Demand-led keyword strategy

Research keywords and topics based on search volume and demand. Submit topic lists for CEO approval before turning them into briefs, keeping the content plan aligned with business priorities.

02 / Foundations

Site structure & technical SEO

Build the site's structure and work with the web developer on technical fixes and new functionality. Address issues as they are identified by the SEO owner or other team members.

03 / Editorial

Editorial operations

Give writers briefs and outlines before drafting. Coordinate reviews, resolve content questions, and keep production aligned with the product and company goals.

04 / Iteration

Continuous optimization

Use available capacity to revisit underperforming content while coordinating technical improvements, content refreshes, and backlink-building activity.
