Systems / Search operations

Good SEO is a connected system.

Search work gets stronger when research, technical foundations, useful content and measurement inform one another. These are the operating ideas behind the projects in this portfolio.

See the system in practice arrow_outwardHow I work
The operating model

From question to iteration.

A practical loop, adapted to the site, team and goals rather than applied as a rigid template.

01

Discover

Understand audience needs, current pages, search demand and the business context.

02

Structure

Map topics to pages, clarify site organization and identify technical blockers.

03

Produce

Coordinate briefs, writers, reviews and implementation so the plan becomes real work.

04

Learn

Review search performance and quality signals, then decide what to maintain or improve.

Systems in the work

Repeatable foundations, tailored execution.

The resume and project work point to a few recurring systems: search planning, technical maintenance, editorial operations and AI-assisted support.

manage_search
