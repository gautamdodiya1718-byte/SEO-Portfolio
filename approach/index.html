A practical working rhythm

Four steps. One feedback loop.

The order is clear, but the process can loop as new information comes in.

01

Understand the context

Learn the offer, audience, site, team and constraints. Review available search and analytics evidence, existing content and the technical setup before settling on a direction.

02

Choose what matters

Bring search demand, intent, page quality and business relevance together. Map opportunities to pages and sequence the work so the team can act on it.

03

Build with the team

Turn priorities into briefs, content, technical recommendations and clear handoffs. Coordinate with writers and developers, review implementation and keep quality checks in the workflow.

04

Learn and refine

Monitor relevant outcomes, inspect pages and query patterns, and separate observation from attribution. Use what the evidence supports to guide the next cycle.

What this looks like day to day

Clear work, careful handoffs.

My experience has included SEO research and planning, technical SEO analysis, content strategy, writer coordination and AI-supported workflows. I try to make the next action understandable to the person doing it.

For content

Start with the audience and search need. Define the page’s purpose, create a useful brief, support production and review for accuracy, usefulness and fit.

For technical SEO

Identify and describe issues in practical terms, agree on priority with the team, coordinate implementation and check that the intended change is in place.

For AI-assisted work

Use workflows to support repeatable drafting and optimization tasks, while keeping human review for factual accuracy, product details and final quality.
